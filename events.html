EventsMichigan · Always current

Worth going to, and who to follow.

When I come across something good, I put it up here. Under that is every Michigan festival, screening series, and crew group that publishes its own dates : so there's always somewhere current to look, whether or not I've been paying attention lately.

How this stays honest. Anything I post carries a date and takes itself down the day after it happens, so you'll never land on a dead listing. When I haven't added anything, the organizations below are still posting their own schedules : they're the ones actually worth following.

Worth Going To

Hand-picked

Festivals & Competitions

Deadlines vary
01

Horror Film Roulette

A timed horror filmmaking competition : you draw constraints, then you go shoot. Kickoff at the Scarab Club, showcase at Emagine Royal Oak. One of the best on-ramps in the state if you've never finished a short.

Trail Dead took Best Editing here in 2025. Can vouch for it.

Metro DetroitHorrorTimed compBeginner friendly Visit ↗ 02

The Comedy Roll

The comedy counterpart : same timed-competition format, kickoff at the Eastern Palace Club, showcase at Emagine Royal Oak. Comedy is brutally hard on a deadline, which is exactly the point.

Lookout placed Top 25 here in 2025.

Royal OakComedyTimed compBeginner friendly Visit ↗ 03

Royal Starr Film Festival

Oakland County's flagship festival, screening at Emagine Birmingham 8 : and they run filmmaker mixers throughout the year, not just during the fest. The mixers are the underrated part.

Screenings & Meetups

Recurring
01

Campfire Film Cooperative

Screening series and film education, frequently at the Scarab Club in Detroit : including multi-part programs like their animation-on-film series. The most consistently programmed thing on this list.

Mograph Mondays Detroit

Motion graphics and design meetup. Adjacent to film rather than squarely in it, which is precisely why it's worth going : different room, different skills, different people.
