Flight safety threshold rules.

0–11 mph : Ideal Flight Window

Minimal gimbal stress, stable tracking, and maximum battery life. Ideal for tight architectural passes and smooth speed-ramped shots.

12–18 mph : Active Compensation

Gimbal stabilization active. Wind buffeting may create slight micro-jitter on telephoto or lightweight sensors. Maintain 25% battery safety buffer for return-to-home against headwind.

18+ mph : Grounded / Hold

High motor draw and thermal strain. Risk of battery sag and runaway drift. Switch to handheld gimbal or indoor set setup.
