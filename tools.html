The terms

Such as they are

All six are free and none of them want anything from you. There is no account to make, no email to hand over and no trial that expires. Saved kits, prompts and checklist progress live in your own browser under localStorage, which also means clearing your site data clears them: that is the trade for never holding your work on a server.

Two of these read a live weather model from Open-Meteo, without an API key and without sending anything about you. The Aspect Ratio Previewer parses your own photos and clips locally through URL.createObjectURL(), so a frame you drop in never crosses the network. If you want the longer version of how any of it works, it is written up on the colophon, and what changed most recently is on What’s New.
