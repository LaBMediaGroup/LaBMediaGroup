The Kit

What we actually own.

Every gear list online is a wishlist or an affiliate farm. This is neither : it's what's actually in the bag and on the machine. Six things below decide how the work gets made; everything else is one tap away, down to the gaff tape.

About the shared tagSome productions combine LaB Media's core kit with equipment shared by regular collaborators. Anything marked Shared is gear that gets used on shoots but isn't owned outright : it felt dishonest to list it without saying so.

No affiliate links, nothing sent free. Prices and verdicts get added as they are checked rather than remembered. Anything not yet written up simply isn’t on the page.
