# Art shed computer

The battery-powered laptop sits on the shed’s existing 0.40 × 0.30 m crate, whose top is 0.26 m above the shed floor. The Lenovo ThinkPad X220i 4287 reference measures 0.305 × 0.2065 m with its standard battery. Lenovo lists a closed thickness of 0.019–0.0346 m. The dark magnesium and plastic casing, paired hinge attachments, 12.5-inch display, keyboard, red pointing stick and touchpad follow the front-view illustration. The screen faces the shed opening. Deck markings and ports are simplified for the field camera. The screen uses a battery-powered display material.
